English

Summary: this app does not collect or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no server, and the app does not use the internet permission.

Data handled only on your device

  • Bluetooth address and name of the paired bed — to reconnect. Deleted when you unpair or uninstall.
  • Preset names and Modes and Routines settings (pre-execution delay, success notification).
  • Bed action history (latest 200 entries), shown in the app.
  • Troubleshooting log files in the app-specific folder (max 5 MB, deleted after 7 days; Bluetooth addresses are partially masked).

Sharing

The app sends no data to the developer or any third party. Log files are shared only when you tap "Export logs" and choose a destination yourself. The "Buy a coffee" donation is an in-app purchase through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google (Google Privacy Policy); the app only marks the purchase as completed so you can donate again, and never receives or stores payment information.

Permissions

Nearby devices (Bluetooth), notifications, full-screen notifications (optional), and a connected-device foreground service — used only for bed control and Samsung Modes and Routines. The app does not use notification access and never reads other apps' notifications.

Children

This app is not directed to children.
